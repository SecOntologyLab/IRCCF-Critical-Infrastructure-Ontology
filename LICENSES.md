## Authorship, attribution and scope

When reusing original IRCCF research contributions, cite the associated IEEE Access article if published, identify the repository and version or commit, and acknowledge the relevant contributors. Citation of a paper is requested for research transparency; the applicable license governs legally required attribution.

The CC BY and MIT notices apply only where the relevant contributors or rights holders have authorised the grant. If you believe a file has been misclassified or contains material not authorised for public distribution, open an issue or contact the repository maintainers before reuse.

## Reproducibility and versioning

The archived Stage 5.3 and Stage 5.4 releases represent preliminary computational research snapshots. Subsequent expert review may result in narrowed, split, replaced or rejected regulatory mappings. Any changes should be tracked as a later release and must not be silently attributed to the published computational baseline.
